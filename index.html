How to Play
  1. Select a building from the palette above (Solar, Trees, Houses, etc.).
  2. Click an empty tile to place it — the timer starts with your first placement!
  3. Click any placed building to remove it and refund its budget!
  4. Build a diverse city: Mix clean energy, homes, nature, and resources for up to a +75 Eco-Diversity Bonus!
  5. Click Stop when you're satisfied (or wait for the timer) to submit your score to the Leaderboard!
